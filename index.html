Meri Shayari

Kuch alfaaz, kuch ehsaas aur kuch ankahi baatein.

01 — Khamoshi

Kuch baatein lafzon mein dhal nahi paati, Kuch yaadein dil se nikal nahi paati. Hum likhte hain apne sukoon ke liye, Har kahani mehfil mein kahi nahi jaati.

— Arshlan Tanha

02 — Insaan

Chehron ki bheed mein pehchaan dhoondhte hain, Khud se door hokar jahaan dhoondhte hain. Kabhi dil ki bhi sun liya karo dosto, Hum har jagah bas zabaan dhoondhte hain.

— Arshlan Tanha

03 — Ek Khayal

Har raaste ki manzil mile, zaroori toh nahi, Har khamoshi mein mehfil mile, zaroori toh nahi. Kuch safar khud ko jaanne ke liye hote hain, Har safar mein humsafar mile, zaroori toh nahi.

— Arshlan Tanha

Shayar Kaun?

Main hoon Arshlan Tanha. Sukhanama meri woh chhoti si duniya hai, jahan mere alfaaz, mere khayalat aur meri nazmein jagah paate hain. Yahan har lafz ek ehsaas hai aur har nazm ek kahani.

"Alfaaz mere hain, magar ehsaas shayad tumhare bhi hon."
